# **Day 23 – Customer Discovery & MVP Blueprint**

## **Overview**

* Defined the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
* Created a Buyer Persona
* Listed major customer pain points
* Studied the customer journey
* Reviewed buying triggers and objections
* Prioritized MVP features using MoSCoW
* Explored pricing assumptions and business risks

## **Key Learnings**

* A clear customer profile leads to better product decisions.
* Prioritization helps avoid feature overload.
* Understanding customer behavior is essential before building an MVP.
* Every startup should identify risks and validate assumptions early.

## **Reflection**

Today reinforced that successful products start with understanding customers, not just writing code.
A well-defined MVP focuses on solving real problems instead of trying to build everything at once.
