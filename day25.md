# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Overview
Participated in an AI-powered Shark Tank simulation by pitching a startup idea,
answering investor questions, and receiving feedback on the business model, funding,
valuation, and overall investment potential.

## Key Learnings

- Structuring a startup pitch.

- Defending business decisions under investor scrutiny.

- Understanding funding requirements and valuation.

- Importance of market validation and competitive differentiation.

- Improving confidence in presenting business ideas.

## Reflection
This activity showed me that entrepreneurship is about more than having an innovative idea.
A successful founder must communicate the vision clearly, justify financial decisions, understand the market,
and adapt based on investor feedback.
