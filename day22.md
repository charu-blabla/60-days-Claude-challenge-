# Day 22 – AI Startup Validation

## Overview
Used an AI-powered startup validation framework to 
analyze a startup idea. The exercise included market
research, customer analysis, competitor analysis, risk assessment,
and a roadmap for validating the idea before development.

## Key Learnings

- Importance of validating an idea before building.
  
- Basics of TAM, SAM, and SOM.
  
- Identifying competitors and market opportunities.
  
- Creating an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
  
- Evaluating risks and planning the next validation steps.
  
## Reflection
This exercise showed me that building a startup involves much more 
than coding. A strong understanding of the market and customer needs 
is essential before starting development.
