## Overview

Today focused on transforming a startup idea into a complete business strategy.
I explored how to validate the business model, define revenue streams, plan customer 
acquisition, develop a go-to-market strategy, and prepare an investor-ready pitch.

## Key Learnings

- Learned how to create and analyze a Business Model Canvas.

- Understood different revenue and pricing strategies.

- Explored go-to-market and customer acquisition planning.

- Learned how to attract the first 100 users.

- Analyzed competitive positioning, moat, and Reverse SWOT Analysis.

- Reviewed the basics of an investor one-liner and founder pitch.


## Reflection

This exercise showed me that building a startup is much more than writing code.
A successful product also requires a clear business strategy, an understanding of customers, 
sustainable revenue planning, and a roadmap for growth. It gave me a broader perspective on how
technical and business skills work together to build successful products.
