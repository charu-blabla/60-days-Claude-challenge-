# Day 30 - Supply Chain Optimizer Simulator

## Overview: 

Built an interactive Supply Chain Optimizer Simulator that demonstrates how strategic
supply chain decisions affect business performance and optimization metrics.


## Key Learnings:

- Explored supplier, manufacturing, warehousing, transportation, and inventory strategies.
  
- Learned the trade-offs between cost, efficiency, and operational risk.
  
- Improved AI-assisted application development using Claude.
  
- Understood how simulations can support business decision-making.

## Reflection:

This project reinforced that supply chains are interconnected systems where every decision
has measurable consequences. Experimenting with different strategies helped me better understand 
optimization and the importance of balancing cost, speed, and resilience.
