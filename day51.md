# Day 51 – Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## Overview:
Today's focus was on laying the foundation for the 10-day capstone project by following the Product Discovery and Sprint Planning process.
Instead of jumping directly into development, the emphasis was on identifying the right problem, defining a realistic project scope,
and planning the complete development journey.

## Key Learnings:
- Product discovery is as important as development.
- A focused MVP is more valuable than an overloaded product.
- Careful planning reduces development risks.
- Clear requirements simplify implementation and testing.

## Reflection: 
Initially, I wanted to build a feature-rich application, but today's discussion helped me realize that a focused 
and well-executed MVP is far more valuable than an unfinished product with too many features. By narrowing the scope 
and planning each development phase carefully, I now have a clear roadmap for the next nine days and greater confidence in delivering a polished final product.
