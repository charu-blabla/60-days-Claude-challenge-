# Day 12: Resume Optimization & Career Readiness Toolkit

## Overview

Explored resume optimization, ATS (Applicant Tracking System) analysis,
job description matching, skill gap assessment, and personal branding strategies.
Learned how to improve career documents using AI-assisted feedback and recommendations.

## Key Takeaways

- Understanding how ATS systems evaluate resumes.

- Matching resumes with job descriptions effectively.

- Identifying skill gaps for target roles.

- Improving personal branding and professional profiles.

- Using AI tools to enhance job search preparation.


## Benefits

- Better resume visibility to recruiters.

- Clear roadmap for skill development.

- Improved job application strategy.

- Stronger professional online presence.


## Reflection

Today helped me understand that technical skills alone are not enough.
Presenting those skills effectively through a well-optimized resume and 
professional profile is equally important for career growth.
