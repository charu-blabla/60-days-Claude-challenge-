# Day 14 - AI-Powered Job Red Flag Detector

## Overview

A web-based Job Red Flag Detector that analyzes job descriptions and company information
to identify potential risks, highlight positive signals, generate interview questions, and provide an overall hiring risk assessment.

## Key Features

* Job Description Analysis
* Company Information Evaluation
* Risk Score Generation
* Red Flag & Positive Signal Detection
* Risk Breakdown Report
* AI-Generated Interview Questions
* Final Verdict & Recommendations

## What I Learned

* How to structure AI-powered analysis workflows
* The importance of evaluating job opportunities beyond salary and title
* Prompt engineering for risk assessment and report generation
* Presenting complex insights in a user-friendly format

### Reflection

This project showed me how AI can assist job seekers in making more informed career decisions by turning raw job data into actionable insights.
