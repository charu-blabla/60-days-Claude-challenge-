# Day 48 — The Verdict Engine

## Overview

For Day 48 of the ABTalks 60-Day Claude Challenge, I worked with Claude to create an interactive Verdict Engine application.
The application allows users to adjust the weights of different decision criteria and observe how the rankings change
dynamically. It also provides transparency into the sources behind the data and explains how research conflicts were resolved.

## Key Learnings

- Changing the weight of different criteria can significantly affect the final ranking. This showed me how interactive
 decision-support systems can help users explore different outcomes based on their priorities.

- A decision is more trustworthy when users can see where the data came from. The source panel helped demonstrate
 the importance of transparency in AI-powered applications.

- The "How this was researched" panel provided insight into how information was gathered and how conflicts between sources were resolved.

- Claude can generate complete functional web applications from well-structured prompts and user feedback.
The process also showed the importance of answering clarification questions to guide the final result.

## Reflection

Day 48 helped me understand how AI-generated applications can be used to create transparent and interactive decision-making tools.
The most interesting part was adjusting the criteria weights and watching the ranking update live. It demonstrated how the outcome
of a decision can change depending on what factors are considered most important.I also appreciated the focus on research transparency.
Showing the sources behind each data point and explaining how conflicting information was handled makes the decision-making process easier to understand and trust.
