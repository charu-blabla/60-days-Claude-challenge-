# Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower Simulator

## Overview

On Day 31 of the ABTalks 60-Day AI Challenge, I created an AI-powered Supply Chain Control
Tower Simulator using Claude. The application simulates real-time supply chain operations by
presenting operational alerts, allowing users to prioritize incidents, choose corrective actions,
and monitor the impact of each decision on business KPIs. The simulator also includes a performance dashboard to evaluate operational effectiveness.

## Key Learnings

- Learned how AI can generate complete interactive HTML applications.
- Understood the importance of prioritizing operational incidents.
- Explored KPI-based decision making in supply chain management.
- Practiced handling multiple simultaneous operational alerts.
- Improved prompt engineering and AI-assisted application development skills.

## Reflection

This project provided practical insight into how AI can support supply chain operations and 
operational decision-making. Building and interacting with the simulator strengthened my
understanding of real-time incident management while continuing to improve my ability to create useful applications with AI.
