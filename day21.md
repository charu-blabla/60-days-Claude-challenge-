# Day 21 – Digital Privacy Dashboard

## Overview:
Created an interactive HTML dashboard to visualize digital privacy metrics,
including Digital Footprint Score, Privacy Score, Exposure Heatmap, Company 
Exposure Ranking, Digital Twin Profile, Risk Radar, Most Valuable Data Assets,
Privacy Improvement Simulator, and Final Verdict.

## Key Learnings:

- Structured complex dashboards using HTML.
  
- Understood how privacy-related data can be represented visually.
  
- Explored concepts like digital footprints, exposure risk, and privacy scoring.
  
- Improved skills in organizing large UI layouts.
  
## Reflection:
This project showed me how web development can be used to present meaningful 
information in a clear and interactive way. It also increased my awareness of 
digital privacy and the importance of protecting personal data online.
