# Day 13: Exploring AI-Powered Job Discovery & Career Analysis

## Overview:
Learned how to set up Claude Connectors, create a professional profile,
define job search criteria, and use AI to discover relevant job opportunities.

## Key Takeaways:

- Understanding AI-assisted job searching
- Creating structured professional profiles
- Defining effective job search filters
= Exploring skill-gap analysis and market insights
= Evaluating job opportunities using match scores

## Benefits:

- More targeted job search process
- Better understanding of industry requirements
- Identification of missing skills to develop
- Improved career planning and decision-making

## Reflection:
This activity showed me how AI can simplify job discovery and provide valuable insights 
about skills, market demand, and career opportunities. It also helped me understand the
importance of maintaining a strong professional profile.
