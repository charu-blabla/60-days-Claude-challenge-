# Day 32 – Marketing Strategy Simulator

## Overview

Built an interactive Marketing Strategy Simulator using Claude. The application helps 
create customized marketing plans for businesses and personal brands by identifying the 
right audience, selecting marketing channels, defining content pillars, generating a 30-day roadmap, and adapting to unexpected marketing events.

## Key Learnings

- Learned how AI can assist in building marketing strategies.

- Understood the importance of defining a target audience.

- Explored different marketing platforms for various audiences.

- Created effective content pillars for long-term growth.

- Generated a structured 30-day marketing roadmap.

- Learned how to respond to changing marketing scenarios.

- Studied reusable Claude prompts for future marketing projects.

## Reflection

This project showed me how AI can be used beyond coding to solve real business problems. 
It strengthened my understanding of strategic marketing, personal branding, and prompt engineering 
while continuing my hands-on learning journey in the ABTalks 60-Day Claude Challenge.
