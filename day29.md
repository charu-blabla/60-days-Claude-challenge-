# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Simulator

## Overview

Today I created Operation Lifeline, an interactive React HTML application generated with Claude.
The simulator places the user in the role of a business leader managing a supply chain crisis
through strategic decisions, supplier negotiations, leadership responses, and AI investment planning.

## Key Learnings

- Understanding supply chain crisis management
- Strategic business decision-making
- Supplier negotiation techniques
- Leadership during operational disruptions
- AI-driven supply chain optimization
- Rapid application development using Claude

## Reflection

This project demonstrated how AI can be used to create realistic business simulations 
that combine technical development with strategic thinking. It also highlighted the importance of
balancing short-term crisis response with long-term investment in AI-powered supply chain resilience.
