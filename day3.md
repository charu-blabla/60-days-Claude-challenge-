## Day 3 – Role-Based Prompting

What I Learned

Today I learned about Role-Based Prompting, a technique where you assign a specific role to an AI before asking a question.

Instead of asking a question directly, you can instruct the AI to act as a Developer, Product Manager, HR Manager, Founder, Marketer, or any other expert. This helps the AI generate responses that are more relevant, detailed, and tailored to a particular perspective.

## Key Takeaways

- Role-Based Prompting gives AI additional context before answering.
- Different roles can produce different responses to the same question.
- Expert roles often provide more focused and practical insights.
- It improves response quality without changing the actual question.

Example

Question:

«How can a startup grow with a limited budget?»

Possible Roles:

- Founder
- Marketer
- Product Manager
- HR Manager
- Developer

Each role approaches the problem differently and offers unique recommendations.

## Benefits of Role-Based Prompting

1. More relevant answers
2. Better quality responses
3. Saves time by reducing the need for follow-up prompts

## Reflection

A small change in how a prompt is written can significantly improve the quality of AI-generated responses. Role-Based Prompting is a simple but powerful technique that helps AI think from the perspective most relevant to the task.
