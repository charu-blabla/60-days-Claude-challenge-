# Day 15 – Astrology & Life Analysis Interface

## Overview:
Built and explored the structure of a detailed astrology and life analysis
dashboard featuring guided user input, organized navigation, and report presentation concepts.

## Key Takeaways:
- Learned how complex information can be organized into user-friendly sections.
- Explored multi-step workflows and navigation patterns.
- Improved understanding of React component-based UI structures.
- Studied effective presentation of large datasets and reports.
  
## Reflection:
Today's work helped me understand how larger applications are planned and structured. 
I gained insight into organizing information, improving user experience, and breaking complex interfaces into manageable components.
