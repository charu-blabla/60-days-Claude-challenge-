# Day 15 – Building a Custom Stock Research Skill in Claude

## Overview:
Created a custom Claude Skill for stock fundamental analysis and company comparison. 
Tested the skill by generating research reports, analyzing valuation metrics, reviewing
ownership trends, identifying risk factors, and exploring how reusable AI workflows can 
streamline financial research without repeatedly entering prompts.

- Creating and configuring custom AI skills in Claude

- Building reusable workflows for stock research

- Generating fundamental analysis reports

- Comparing multiple stocks efficiently

- Understanding valuation, ownership, and risk analysis

- Improving productivity through prompt reusability

## Reflection:
Building a custom Claude skill taught me how resuable AI workflow can automate complex
tasks. I learned how to generate stock research reports more efficiently and reduce repetitive
prompting while improving consistency in analysis.
